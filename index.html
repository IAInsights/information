What is the Operations Hub?

The Operations Hub brings together the main Globe Travel Retail tools into one Cockpit. Instead of searching through different links and platforms, Operations teams can use one central access point to find the information they need.

The objective is simple: make daily follow-up easier, improve visibility and give teams faster access to operational and reporting information.
